Starlet Swarm

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026
Android package: com.neaura.starletswarm

This policy describes how information is handled when you play Starlet Swarm, a game by Neaura ("we", "us", or "our"), and when you contact us for support.

1. Game data on your device

The game stores progress, unlocked content, in-game currency, and sound preferences locally on your device so that you can continue playing. The game does not require you to create an account. These local save files are not uploaded to a developer-operated game server by the game's save system.

2. Advertising and information collected by Google

The game integrates Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display banner and interstitial advertisements. When the SDK operates, Google collects and shares information used for advertising, advertising measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. This includes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID, where available.
  • Interactions with the app and advertisements, such as app launches, ad views, and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, including crash logs and performance information.

The data processed depends on the SDK version, device settings, advertising configuration, and applicable consent choices. Google processes this information under its own policies. Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

3. Support communications

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

4. Sharing and processing

The advertising SDK sends the information described above to Google and its advertising services. Support messages are processed through our email provider. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect users and address fraud or security incidents. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, as described in their policies.

5. Retention and deletion

Local game data is retained on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Clearing storage permanently resets local progress. Any operating-system backups must be managed separately through your device or backup provider.

Support messages are kept while needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or to meet legal obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence using the contact address below; some information may need to be retained when legally required. Data collected by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies; we cannot directly erase Google's independent records.

6. Your choices

You can manage or delete your advertising ID and adjust available advertising privacy controls in your Android or Google account settings. Available options depend on your Android version and region. You can stop further collection by the game and its SDK by uninstalling the app. For questions or requests concerning data handled by Google, see Google's privacy controls and request options.

7. Security

Local saves are stored within the app's storage area. Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal information through the game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information to us through support, please contact us so that we can investigate and address it. The advertising data practices described above also need to be considered when deciding whether the game is appropriate for a child.

9. This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include advertising, analytics scripts, forms, or tracking cookies added by us. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about Starlet Swarm, contact Neaura at peterthachho@gmail.com. Please include "Starlet Swarm Privacy" in the subject.
